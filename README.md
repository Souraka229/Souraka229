<img src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/devicons/devicon/icons/bash/bash-original.svg" width="28" height="28" alt=""/> Manifesto

The gap between global tech and African business reality isn't a bug.
It's the product brief.
Most platforms treat Africa as an afterthought:
  ✗ No design for how commerce actually moves on the ground
  ✗ Weak or missing local payment rails
  ✗ Tools that assume always-on laptop workflows
I'm not building FOR Africa from outside.
I'm building FROM INSIDE — designing around constraints, not past them.
A restaurant in Cotonou shouldn't need a laptop farm to accept payments.
A migrant shouldn't need a tech degree to understand their case status.
An SME shouldn't need venture capital for a professional online presence.
When you build from inside the constraint,
you don't only create solutions for Africa —
you create products the world eventually follows.
"The best opportunities aren't reserved for the right backgrounds.
They're waiting for the right conviction."
🌍 Young. African. Tech-driven. Apply anyway.
The world is watching — and it's building here.
